# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OSIEL Labs aims to maintain a respectful, constructive engineering environment where participants can collaborate without harassment, discrimination or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate professionally and assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Critique ideas and code, not individuals.
- Respect differing backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.
- Protect confidential information and respect consent and privacy.
- Welcome clear, constructive feedback and acknowledge mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, deliberate humiliation, repeated personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of confidential material without permission are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use a repository's private security or maintainer contact mechanisms, where available, for conduct reports. Do not include sensitive personal details in public issues. Organization maintainers may remove content, limit participation or restrict access to protect the community.

This policy applies to OSIEL Labs-managed public project spaces and related official interactions.
